## Notes

- Split the backlog by source, repo, and workflow layers.
- Prefer outcome-oriented entries over vague “add skill” placeholders.
- Every entry should have an owner or an explicitly unowned status that needs resolution.
- Check `no_skill_gap` before creating a new skill.
- Merge into an existing skill by default when the trigger and owner overlap.
- Promote upstream only after private company facts have been removed and the method is reusable.
