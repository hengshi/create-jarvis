5. probes only those supplied pointers, then confirms unresolved history range, publication target and delivery policy;

7. creates the Construction Workspace and runs Jarvis repo initialization first;
8. coordinates Jarvis construction and one independent learning card per code repository;
9. continues through reconciliation and route-scoped workflow construction;
10. stops only for real authorization, business ambiguity, Git approval, unavailable future supervised-runtime evidence, or an external blocker;

The customer does not receive phases or child-task startup commands. When the journey pauses, provide the exact Construction Workspace path and recovery phrase.
